DOGA 2025. The Battle for My Identity

English edition · 1st edition

Author: Olmo Gómez Aldaz.
Publisher: Genus Homo.
Published in February 2026.

First-person account of an identity theft and fraudulent adoption that took place in Bilbao, Spain, in 1971. The book originated as a formal declaration submitted to the Spanish State under the Democratic Memory framework and later became a public text combining personal testimony, documentary evidence and legal proceedings. It reconstructs the author’s life history, the concealment of his origins and the path that led to the judicial restitution of his biological filiation, including the final court rulings and their consequences.
